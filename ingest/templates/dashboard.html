Payer Claims Lakehouse

From raw claim files to PMPM, utilization, and readmissions, with every step gated and tested

A member-level claims lakehouse on the CMS synthetic Medicare files. Enrollment and claims arrive in yearly batches, pass data contracts, land in versioned bronze, and are built into silver and gold with dbt on DuckDB. This page is generated from the gold marts and from the pipeline's own load log and test results.

Synthetic data. CMS generated these files with Synthea and warns they have very limited inferential value; payments and lengths of stay do not resemble real Medicare. The rates below show how the measures are built and tested. They are not findings about Medicare, hospitals, or members.

Why stays are not index stays

Each stay gets the first readmission exclusion that applies; the rest are index stays. Discharge status is always 1 (home) in this source, so the death, against-advice, and transfer exclusions never fire here. An end-to-end test injects those codes to exercise them.

How well the source's codes conform

Diagnoses and ICD procedures are checked against the ICD-10 code set of each claim's fiscal year; revenue-line HCPCS codes are checked for CPT Category I or HCPCS Level II format (no CPT descriptions are stored). Problems are measured, not quarantined, because they are systemic in this source.

Data health

Every arrival passes its data contract before it reaches bronze, and every model is built and tested by dbt build; an error-level test failure stops the run. Each mart is recomputed from silver in a reconciliation test.

How to read this

  • Synthetic, not real. Nothing here describes Medicare beneficiaries. Counts describe the source files.
  • Denominators are fee-for-service-eligible member-months in the claims window, and a stay counts in its admission month. 2015 starts in March and 2023 ends in February; the denominator covers the same months, so their rates stay comparable.
  • Readmissions are crude. No risk adjustment and no planned-readmission algorithm (75% of ICD procedure fields hold diagnosis codes), and rates are member-level, not attributed to hospitals.
  • Stays outside the denominator (for example, members with Part A only) are reported next to PMPM in the table, not dropped silently.
